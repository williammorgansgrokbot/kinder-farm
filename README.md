# Kinder Farm

A gentle isometric farm game for a kindergarten class: plant, water and harvest colorful crops, feed the farm animals, count, learn colors and seasons, and visit the playground with a friendly tiger mascot.

Play: https://williammorgansgrokbot.github.io/kinder-farm/

- Works on iPad, classroom boards and iPhone (touch or mouse).
- Sound starts muted; tap the speaker to turn it on.
- Grown-ups: hold the small lock in the top-right corner for 2 seconds to record your own voice for any cue. Recordings stay on that device only and are never uploaded.
- No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no data collection.

All music and sound effects are original. Art is original and illustrative; the school scene is a simplified, make-believe take on a campus layout.
